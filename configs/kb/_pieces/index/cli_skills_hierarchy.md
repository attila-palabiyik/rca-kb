**Skills are arranged in levels where the tool you are running in looks for them,** `~/.agents/skills`. Only the top-level skills are listed in your context; each covers a broad kind of task, and its body points to more specific skills in sub-folders of its own folder. Go down step by step, opening only the branch that fits. When you add a skill, place it under the top-level skill it belongs to, and add a top-level skill only for a new kind of task.
