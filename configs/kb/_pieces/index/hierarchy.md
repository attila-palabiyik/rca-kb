**Entries are arranged in levels.** `/kb/AGENTS.md` lists the top-level groups and has already been loaded into your context; each group is a folder under `/kb/entries` with its own `INDEX.md` listing its entries and any sub-groups. Go down level by level, opening only the branch that fits your task. When you add an entry, place it in the group it belongs to, and split a group that has grown too long to scan.
