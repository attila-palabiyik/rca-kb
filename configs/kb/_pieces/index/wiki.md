**Pages are separate files** under `/kb/entries` that link to related pages. `/kb/AGENTS.md` lists every page on a single line with a one-line description and has already been loaded into your context, and `/kb/entries/LOG.md` records each change to the wiki in one line. Open the pages that bear on your task and follow their links. When you add or revise a page, update its line in the list, link it to and from related pages, and add a line to the log.
