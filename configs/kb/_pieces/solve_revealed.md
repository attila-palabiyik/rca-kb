## Knowledge base

A knowledge base built from earlier debugging tasks is available at `/kb`. An agent like you wrote it. After each of those tasks it was shown how its answer had been graded and what the correct fix was, and it wrote with that in front of it. Those were different designs with different bugs, so judge for yourself what carries over to this one. It may be empty.

Look at it before you start work on the task, using the conventions below.
